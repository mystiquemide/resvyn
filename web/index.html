The signer is immutable — bound once at deploy, no setter. Only its signature settles a claim, and the decision is bound to the exact claim, coverage, claimant, evidence hash, amount, and chain. A decision for one claim cannot be replayed onto another.

Negative proofs

Both enforced at the contract level as call-level rejections. No transaction, no state change, no balance moved.
NonceAlreadyUsed Replaying the exact signed decision that already settled claim #1 reverts. One decision can pay once. The settlement nonce is burned and terminal.
InsufficientFreeReserve Issuing coverage for one wei more than the free reserve (lock 0.004000000000000001 against 0.004 free) reverts. This is the invariant itself, enforced to the wei.

verifiable live These paths are re-checkable now via eth_call against the deployed contract; a simulated call reverts with the exact custom error and changes nothing.

Reproduce

One command drives the entire ceremony: deploy, deposit, issue, claim, AI-signed resolve, reclaim, plus both negative proofs.

RESVYN_RPC_URL=https://rpc.botchain.ai RESVYN_ALLOW_MAINNET=i-understand \
RESVYN_DEPOSIT=0.005 RESVYN_MAX_PAYOUT=0.001 RESVYN_APPROVE_AMOUNT=0.001 \
RESVYN_RECLAIM=1 npx tsx scripts/rehearse.ts

Contract source contracts/WarrantyReserve.sol, 100% line/statement coverage, 95 passing tests. The proof already fired once (2026-08-10); re-running deploys a fresh instance, so the command is for auditors, not required to trust this page — the live reads above stand on their own.
